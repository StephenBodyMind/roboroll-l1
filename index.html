Founder story

Created from pain — and from one unforgettable moment of relief.

Dr. Stephen, RoboRoll's inventor and a Ph.D. in Robotics, lived with eight herniated discs and severe back fasciitis. He tried massage chairs, cushions, and nearly every massager he could find. Most of them did the same thing: vibrate, knead, and follow a fixed factory routine.

Then a physical therapist used a simple roller along his spine — pressing, supporting, rolling, and releasing. The feeling was completely different from ordinary massage: the tight fascia began to open, the over-curved back felt gently supported, and the relief came from deep inside.

That moment became the question behind RoboRoll: why can't a robot bring this therapist-like rolling care home?

We are building a robotic therapist for everyday back care.

The first prototype was built in a university lab. Then came two years of product development: biomechanics, physical therapy principles, discs, fascia, nerves, AI routines, dozens of redesigns, and thousands of tests. Our goal is not the flashiest machine. It is a precise, accessible product that can help millions wake up feeling a little lighter.
