Founder story

Created from pain, engineered for relief.

RoboRoll began with real back pain: eight herniated discs, severe back fasciitis, and the frustration of trying massage chairs, cushions, and devices that mostly vibrated, kneaded, and followed fixed routines.

Then one physical therapist used a simple roller along the spine: pressing, supporting, rolling, releasing. The feeling was different from ordinary massage. The relief came from deep inside.

Why can’t a robot bring this therapist-like rolling care home?
